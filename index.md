Hello ! I am Prasanna Maddila, a PhD student at the [University of Toulouse](https://www.univ-tlse3.fr), at the [MIAT](https://miat.inrae.fr) Lab, INRAE. I'm supervised by [Régis Sabbadin](https://miat.inrae.fr/member/sabbadinregis/) and [Meritxell Vinyals](https://sites.google.com/site/meritxellvinyals/). Before that, I did my Masters in Engineering at [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/en/), Paris, where I studied Computer Science (Sciences du Logiciel).

In my PhD work, I study Nash Equilibrium computation in multi-agent games, either by exact methods (such as approximation schemes), or by Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning techniques.
